New York City is home to some of the most celebrated museums in the world, offering a rich tapestry of art, history, science, and culture. From iconic institutions like The Met and MoMA to immersive experiences at the Tenement Museum and the American Museum of Natural History, the city’s museums capture the spirit and diversity of human creativity and experience. Whether you’re a first-time visitor or a lifelong New Yorker, exploring these cultural landmarks is an unforgettable journey through time and imagination.
