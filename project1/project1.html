Statue of Liberty

The Statue of Liberty is a colossal neoclassical sculpture on Liberty Island in New York Harbor in New York City, in the United States.

Central Park

Central Park is an urban park in New York City located between the Upper West Side and Upper East Side neighborhoods of Manhattan.

Empire State Building

The Empire State Building is a 102-story Art Deco skyscraper in Midtown Manhattan, New York City, in the state of New York, in the United States.
