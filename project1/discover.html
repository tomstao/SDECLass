Famous Landmarks

From the towering Empire State Building to the majestic Statue of Liberty, explore NYC’s most legendary landmarks.

Statue of Liberty

A gift from France in 1886, the Statue of Liberty stands as a universal symbol of freedom and democracy. Located on Liberty Island, it has welcomed generations of immigrants to the United States.

Empire State Building

Once the tallest building in the world, the Empire State Building is an Art Deco masterpiece offering panoramic views of New York City from its observation decks on the 86th and 102nd floors.

Brooklyn Bridge

Completed in 1883, the Brooklyn Bridge is a marvel of 19th-century engineering. Its iconic suspension design connects Manhattan and Brooklyn, offering scenic views to pedestrians and cyclists.

Times Square

Known as “The Crossroads of the World,” Times Square dazzles with neon lights, digital billboards, Broadway theaters, and buzzing energy — a true heart of New York’s entertainment scene.

The Metropolitan Museum of Art (The Met)

One of the world’s largest and most prestigious art museums, The Met houses over two million works from ancient times to modern masterpieces. Located on Fifth Avenue, it’s a must-visit for art and history lovers.

Central Park

Stretching over 840 acres in the heart of Manhattan, Central Park is a green oasis amid the city’s hustle. With winding paths, scenic lakes, open lawns, and landmarks like Bethesda Terrace and Strawberry Fields, it’s the perfect escape for relaxation, recreation, and iconic New York views.
